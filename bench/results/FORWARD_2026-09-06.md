## What the evidence actually says

Times below are milliseconds relative to scripted person turn 4 beginning. The user resumes at
2841ms, after a 900ms pause, and finishes around 4661ms.

| Event | Candidate run 1 | Candidate run 5 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Person resumes | 2841 | 2841 |
| Prefix final: “Send the confirmation to my email.” | 3108 | 3056 |
| First recorded agent audio starts | 3282 | 3236 |
| First recorded agent segment ends | 3882 | 3736 |
| Person ends | 4661 | 4660 |
| Continuation final: “Actually, make that a text message.” | 5924 | 5840 |

All five baseline runs instead produced one full turn-4 final at 6134–6284ms. Three other candidate
runs also produced one full final. Failed runs' prefix finals arrived **215–267ms after resume**;
recorded audio started **174–180ms after that final**. These observations do not show playback
before commitment. Aggregate eotMs/TTFT/yield durations have different anchors and cannot establish
that ordering; the raw event timeline can.

The split makes the mock's history-count selector advance from response 4 to response 5, then to
its fallback. That explains four off-script replies, not four independent acoustic failures.
This is a benchmark-contract failure and a real early turn boundary, but does not establish that
an unscripted LLM would answer parking to a text-message request. No tool actually ran in this rig.

### Ruled out / not established

- **Not the naive bootstrap window bug.** The tested candidate accumulates energy over exactly
  4096 samples before updating VAD counters (`candidate.patch`, `_bootstrapVadTick`). The current
  4096-packet source is the restored baseline, not evidence of what the candidate ran.
- **Not a VAD-forced commit in the native-EOT path.** `_endOfSpeech()` returns immediately for
  `stt.nativeEOT`; Deepgram's adapter sets that flag and turns `TurnInfo/EndOfTurn` into `onFinal`.
- **No demonstrated speculative playback/gate escape.** Presynthesis only warms a clip. The
  candidate did not change `onFinal` or `_takePrefetch` commitment checks; timeline audio follows
  the prefix final. New diagnostic tests also confirm no playback/history change on speculation.
- **Provider-level reason remains unknown.** Saved data contains adapter finals and audio, not
  complete provider TurnInfo/turn_index/confidence/audio-position telemetry. Native EndOfTurn is
  the code-path explanation; we cannot reconstruct the provider's internal decision or assert a
  transport root cause from these recordings alone.

Add BENCH-ONLY telemetry before running: incoming raw Flux TurnInfo event/type/turn_index plus

The presynth-only arm is the lower-coupling candidate, NOT presumed safe until live validation.

## Fix options after evidence

If a prefix final lands after verified resumption, consider keeping it provisional and combining
it with the continuation BEFORE authorizing a reply/tool. But do not simply drop any final while
`_speaking`: VAD can lag real speech-end or stay active on echo, losing legitimate turns. A safe
policy needs a resume generation, provider turn/audio-span provenance where available, a bounded
completion path, stop/hold/empty-final cleanup and correct echo behavior. Test true interruptions,
short yes/no answers, late callbacks, stalled providers and turn liveness explicitly.

Do not blindly add a universal pause delay: it buys hesitation tolerance by giving back latency on
every answer. Do not infer action reversibility from TTS cancellation. A tool committed from the
prefix cannot necessarily be undone; existing toolGate protects speculation, not provider mistakes.

Promotion requires the original accuracy gate plus explicit premature-turn/action counts, not just
input WER. Once hesitation passes, rerun clean+echo and check p95/stalls. The prior 26% clean gain
is a promising measurement, not a shipped improvement or permission to weaken the gate.
